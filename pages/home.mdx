We are a loose collective who make a lot of open-source game-things.

Our main project is [null0](/null0), a small fantasy console where a game is a wasm "cart" you can write in any of 23 languages, and run in a browser or natively.

You can find us on [github](https://github.com/notnullgames). [konsumer](https://github.com/konsumer), [RobLoach](https://github.com/RobLoach) and [twuky](https://github.com/twuky) make stuff.
